## Install (Windows 10/11)
1. Download **JobDesk-Setup.exe** below.
2. Open it. If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the app is free and doesn't have a paid publisher certificate).
3. Click **Install**. Job Desk opens in your browser and guides you through the rest.

You need a Claude Pro or Max plan. Full guide: [docs/INSTALL.md](https://github.com/achrabieh/job-desk/blob/develop/docs/INSTALL.md)

Already installed? Run the new setup file to update. Your CV, settings and history are kept.
