# Getting a small group started

Goal: learn in 2–3 weeks whether people keep using Job Desk and whether it produces interviews. Keep it to 3–5 people you can help in person.

## Who to invite
- Actively looking or open to a move in the next 3 months.
- On Windows, with (or willing to get) a Claude Pro or Max plan. No technical skills needed.
- Mix: at least one person outside strategy/consulting, and one outside Saudi Arabia, to test the generic parts.

## Setup (they can do it alone; 15 minutes)
Send them the link to [docs/INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md), or straight to the [installer](https://github.com/achrabieh/job-desk/releases/latest/download/JobDesk-Setup.exe). The guided setup covers Claude sign-in, their CV, goals, questions, the master CV check, companies and the first search. Offer a 10-minute call if they get stuck; the usual snag is the Windows "More info → Run anyway" warning.

## What to tell them
- It finds 4–5 jobs a day and never applies for them. They submit, then press "I submitted it".
- Only facts from their fact bank reach a CV. If a card says "Check N items", read them before submitting.
- Press Skip with a reason: that's how it learns. Mark interviews and offers: that's how we measure it.

## Weekly check-in (5 minutes each)
Ask them for a screenshot of the numbers at the top of their Job Desk page, plus answers to:
1. Days you ran it this week? If fewer than 4, why?
2. Best pick this week, and the worst one. Why?
3. Did you edit a tailored CV before sending? What did you change?
4. Anything that felt wrong, slow or confusing?
5. Would you pay for this if it ran in the cloud without your PC? How much a month?

## Bar to move to Stage 1 (hosted)
- 3 of 5 still running it in week 3, at least 4 days a week.
- Approval rate above 30% (picks are relevant).
- At least 2 interviews across the group credited to Job Desk picks.
- At least 2 people say they would pay, with a number.

If the bar isn't met, fix the biggest complaint first and run another two weeks before building anything hosted.
